Verileri Nasıl Kullanıyoruz

  • Günlük astroloji içgörüleri ve YZ rehberliği üretmek

  • Uygulama performansını iyileştirmek

Verilerinizi reklam amacıyla kullanmıyor, üçüncü taraflara satmıyor ve ticari amaçlı paylaşmıyoruz.

Subscription Data

Premium subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store. No card or payment information is transmitted to Neyora. The RevenueCat SDK manages your subscription status (active/inactive, plan type) and does not process payment details.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Location (GPS), camera/microphone, contacts, photos, health data, fingerprint / face data, advertising identifier (IDFA), browsing history.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app functionality (sign-in, personal content, chat)
  • To generate daily astrology insights and AI guidance
  • To verify subscription status and grant premium feature access
  • To improve app performance

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it for commercial purposes.
