# Neyora Support Welcome to Neyora Support. We're here to help with any questions, feedback, or issues you may encounter while using the app. ## Contact Us For support inquiries, bug reports, subscription issues, or general feedback, please contact: **Email:** [support@neyora.app](mailto:support@neyora.app) We typically respond within 1–3 business days. --- ## Frequently Asked Questions ### How do I create an account? You can create an account using your email address directly within the Neyora app. ### How do I restore my Premium subscription? Open the app and navigate to the Premium section, then tap **Restore Purchases**. ### I purchased Premium but don't have access. Please use the Restore Purchases option first. If the issue persists, contact us at [support@neyora.app](mailto:support@neyora.app) and include your purchase details. ### How do I delete my account? You can delete your account from within the app's settings section. If you experience any issues, contact [support@neyora.app](mailto:support@neyora.app). ### How are my personal data handled? Please refer to our Privacy Policy for detailed information regarding data collection, storage, and usage. --- ## Report a Problem When reporting a problem, please include: * Device model * iOS version * App version * Description of the issue * Screenshots (if available) This helps us resolve issues more quickly. --- ## Feedback We welcome suggestions and feedback that help improve Neyora. You can send your ideas and feature requests to: [support@neyora.app](mailto:support@neyora.app) --- Thank you for using Neyora.
